# Loan Eligibility Guidelines

> Document type: Synthetic reference policy
> Policy domain: Eligibility
> Version: 1.0
> Note: This document is fictional and created for the HDFC Loan Intelligence demonstration system.

## Overview

Loan eligibility is assessed using multiple factors rather than a single criterion. The assessment should consider the applicant's income, existing financial obligations, credit history, employment status, requested loan amount, loan purpose, and verification status.

Meeting one eligibility condition does not guarantee loan approval.

## Minimum Eligibility Conditions

An applicant should generally satisfy the following baseline conditions:

- Applicant must be at least 21 years old at the time of application.
- Applicant should have a stable and verifiable source of income.
- Applicant must provide required identity and financial documentation.
- Applicant should have a verifiable credit history where applicable.
- The requested loan amount should be reasonable relative to verified income and repayment capacity.
- Existing financial obligations must be considered when assessing repayment capacity.

## Income Assessment

Income assessment should consider:

- Applicant monthly income.
- Co-applicant monthly income where applicable.
- Annual household income.
- Existing monthly EMI obligations.
- Regular household expenses.
- Stability of employment or business income.

Income should be based on information that can be reasonably verified.

## Credit Assessment

Credit history is an important component of eligibility.

The assessment should consider:

- CIBIL or equivalent credit score.
- Previous loan repayment history.
- Number of previous loans.
- Recorded defaults.
- Current outstanding obligations.
- Length and quality of credit history.

A strong credit score does not automatically result in approval if other financial indicators are unfavorable.

## Loan Amount Assessment

The requested loan amount should be assessed against:

- Verified annual income.
- Existing obligations.
- Debt-to-income ratio.
- Loan-to-income ratio.
- Loan tenure.
- Purpose of the loan.
- Applicant's overall financial profile.

A high requested amount relative to income may require additional review.

## Manual Review Conditions

Manual review is recommended when:

- The applicant has a history of loan defaults.
- Debt-to-income ratio is unusually high.
- The applicant has no stable income source.
- Important financial information cannot be verified.
- There are conflicting application details.
- The requested loan amount appears disproportionately high relative to income.
- Required documentation is incomplete.

## Important Limitation

Eligibility guidelines are screening criteria and should not be interpreted as an automatic approval or rejection rule.

Final lending decisions require appropriate human review and institutional processes.
