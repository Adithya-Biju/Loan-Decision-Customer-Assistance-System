# General Loan FAQs

> Document type: Synthetic reference policy
> Policy domain: General FAQ
> Version: 1.0

## Does a high CIBIL score guarantee loan approval?

No.

A strong CIBIL score is a positive credit indicator, but approval also depends on income, repayment capacity, existing obligations, employment, requested loan amount, documentation, and other applicable criteria.

## Is a low CIBIL score an automatic rejection?

No.

A low CIBIL score indicates increased credit risk and may require additional review. The complete financial and credit profile should be considered.

## What is DTI?

DTI, or debt-to-income ratio, compares existing monthly EMI obligations with monthly income.

A higher DTI generally indicates a greater existing debt burden.

## Why is a high DTI a concern?

A high DTI means a larger portion of income is already committed to debt repayment.

This can reduce the applicant's available repayment capacity for additional borrowing.

## What is loan-to-income ratio?

Loan-to-income ratio compares the requested loan amount with annual household income.

It provides an indication of how large the requested loan is relative to income.

## Can an unemployed person apply for a loan?

An unemployed applicant may be assessed using alternative verified income, co-applicant income, assets, and other financial information.

Unemployment can trigger additional review.

## Does having previous loans hurt eligibility?

Not necessarily.

Previous loans can demonstrate established credit history when repayments were handled responsibly.

Defaults or poor repayment behavior are more concerning.

## Does a previous default mean automatic rejection?

Not necessarily.

A default is a significant negative factor, but the complete circumstances and current financial position should be assessed.

## Why might an application require manual review?

Manual review may be required when:

- Risk indicators are high.
- Financial information is incomplete.
- Documentation is missing.
- Verification fails.
- Application information conflicts.
- Existing defaults are identified.
- Repayment capacity is uncertain.

## Does a guarantor guarantee approval?

No.

A guarantor may provide additional support, but the applicant and overall loan structure still need to satisfy applicable requirements.

## What happens if documentation is incomplete?

The application may be delayed or returned for additional documentation.

Incomplete documentation should not automatically be interpreted as rejection.

## Can the AI assistant approve my loan?

No.

The AI assistant provides analysis, policy-based information, and recommendations for human review. It must not represent its output as a final lending decision.
