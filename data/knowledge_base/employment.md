# Employment Eligibility Guidelines

> Document type: Synthetic reference policy
> Policy domain: Employment
> Version: 1.0
> Note: This document is fictional and created for the HDFC Loan Intelligence demonstration system.

## Overview

Employment status is an important indicator of income stability and repayment capacity.

The assessment should distinguish between salaried, self-employed, retired, unemployed, and other employment categories.

## Salaried Applicants

Salaried applicants should generally have:

- Verifiable employment.
- Stable income.
- Sufficient employment history.
- Documentation supporting reported income.

Longer employment tenure may provide stronger evidence of income stability.

## Self-Employed Applicants

Self-employed applicants should provide sufficient evidence of business income.

Relevant factors include:

- Business continuity.
- Reported income.
- Business documentation.
- Banking activity.
- Tax records where applicable.

Self-employed applicants may require additional documentation compared with salaried applicants.

## Retired Applicants

Retired applicants may be assessed using:

- Pension income.
- Other verified recurring income.
- Existing financial obligations.
- Age and applicable loan tenure.

## Unemployed Applicants

Applicants without current employment should receive additional scrutiny.

The reviewer should assess:

- Alternative verified income.
- Co-applicant income.
- Existing assets.
- Existing obligations.
- Credit history.
- Repayment capacity.

Unemployment should not automatically be treated as an automatic rejection rule in this reference framework, but it may trigger manual review.

## Employment Stability

Employment length should be considered alongside the applicant's overall financial profile.

A recent job change does not automatically imply poor creditworthiness.

## Verification

Employment information should be verified when required.

Conflicting employment information should be escalated rather than resolved through assumptions.
