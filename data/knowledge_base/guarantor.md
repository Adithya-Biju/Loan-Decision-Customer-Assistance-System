# Guarantor Requirements

> Document type: Synthetic reference policy
> Policy domain: Guarantor
> Version: 1.0
> Note: This document is fictional and created for the HDFC Loan Intelligence demonstration system.

## Overview

A guarantor may be required when additional repayment support or risk mitigation is considered necessary.

The requirement depends on the loan product, applicant profile, and applicable lending process.

## When a Guarantor May Be Considered

A guarantor may be considered when:

- Applicant repayment capacity requires additional support.
- Credit history presents additional risk.
- Income stability is limited.
- The loan structure requires additional assurance.
- The relevant loan product specifies a guarantor requirement.

## Guarantor Assessment

A guarantor should be assessed independently.

Relevant factors may include:

- Identity verification.
- Income verification.
- Credit history.
- Existing financial obligations.
- Relationship to the applicant where relevant.

## Guarantor Does Not Guarantee Approval

The presence of a guarantor does not automatically result in loan approval.

The applicant's own eligibility and the overall loan assessment remain relevant.

## Co-Signer Versus Guarantor

A co-signer and guarantor may have different legal and financial responsibilities depending on the applicable loan agreement.

The system should not provide legal interpretations unless supported by an authoritative policy or agreement.

## Manual Review

Cases involving guarantor requirements should be escalated when:

- Guarantor information is incomplete.
- Financial information cannot be verified.
- Applicant and guarantor information conflicts.
- The applicable product-specific requirement is unclear.
